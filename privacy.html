Privacy Policy – Sam Rivera Clips

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Sam Rivera Clips ("the App") is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Sam Rivera social media accounts.

2. Data we access

  • TikTok basic profile (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm the connected account.
  • Posting permissions (video.upload, video.publish): used only to upload and publish clips to the owner's own account.
  • Publish results: post IDs and status, stored to avoid duplicate posts and to produce a daily activity report.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not access, collect or store data about other TikTok users, followers, messages or viewing activity. We do not sell or share any data.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted by our integration provider and are never published. Operational data is kept in a private repository and deleted when no longer needed.

5. Your choices

The account owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions). Upon revocation, the App can no longer access the account.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or content removal requests: samrivera17t@gmail.com
